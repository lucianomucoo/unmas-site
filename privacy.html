Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This explains what the app stores about you, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

Your email and password (the password is stored encrypted by our login provider), and what you enter: foods you log, your own foods, recipes and meals, goals, profile (sex, birth year, height, activity, goal), weigh-ins, water and body measurements, and your app settings.

Why

Only to run the app for you: to show your log, work out your targets and keep your data on every phone you log in on. We don’t sell your data. Free accounts see ads (see “Ads” below); your food, weight, body and health data are never used for ads.

Where it is kept

In a database run by Supabase. Each account can only read its own data. Some choices (theme, reminders, remembered tabs) stay only on your phone.

Food searches

When you search for a food, the words you type are sent to our own food database and to Open Food Facts to find matches. Open Food Facts doesn’t receive your name or email.

Barcodes and label photos

When you scan a barcode, only its number is sent to Open Food Facts to look the product up; your own foods linked to a barcode are found in your account first. If a product isn’t found, you can photograph its nutrition label: the photo is made smaller on your phone and sent to an AI service (currently Anthropic’s Claude) only to read the numbers. We never save the photo, and it is deleted from your phone right after reading. The AI service doesn’t use it to train its models and may keep the request only for a limited time to prevent misuse. We keep a note that a reading happened (when, and which AI service) to apply a limit of 10 readings a day; it is deleted with your account. You can always type the values yourself instead.

Meal photos and exercise descriptions

If you photograph a meal, the photo is made smaller on your phone and sent to the same AI service only to name the foods and estimate their amounts. If you describe exercise in a sentence, only that sentence is sent, to name the activities. We never save the photo or the sentence on our servers, and the AI service doesn’t use them to train its models (it may keep the request only for a limited time to prevent misuse). When you add the foods from a meal photo, a small copy of the photo is kept ON YOUR PHONE ONLY, next to those foods and in your Photo log; it is deleted when you delete those foods, after 90 days, or when you delete your account. We keep a note that an estimate happened (when, and which AI service) to apply a daily limit; it is deleted with your account.

Ads

Free accounts see a few small ads, and can choose to watch a short ad for a free AI photo. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show them, Google may collect your phone’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks), your rough location from your internet address, and how you interact with the ads. Your food, weight, body and health data are NEVER sent to Google or used for ads. If you don’t allow tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Users under 18 never see the tracking question and only get non-personalised ads rated for teens. We block ads for weight-loss and diet products, gambling, alcohol, dating and cosmetic procedures. Unmas Plus has no ads. Google’s own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Subscriptions

Unmas Plus is paid through your Apple account; we never see your card details. To know who has Plus on every phone you use, we use RevenueCat: it receives your account’s user ID and your purchase history (which plan, when, until when) — not your food or health data. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your App Store settings; refunds are handled by Apple.

Your choices

You can export your data (Settings → Account and data → Export your data) and delete your account and all its data at any time (Settings → Account and data → Delete account). You can allow or stop ad tracking at any time in your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law asks for it, you can change your ad consent in Settings → Account and data → Privacy choices.
