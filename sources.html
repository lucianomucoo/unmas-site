Data sources and credits

Last updated: 26 October 2026

USDA FoodData Central

U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central. Public domain (fdc.nal.usda.gov).

Open Food Facts

Product data from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Albanian Food Composition Table

Hoxha L. (2022). Albanian Food Composition Table. Agricultural University of Tirana. Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16911605). Changes: nutrients the table leaves empty were added by this app from the closest matching food in USDA, BLS, Ciqual, CoFID or CNF (marked “from …” in Full nutrition); the table’s own values are unchanged. Traditional dish values marked “estimate” were calculated by this app from recipes and ingredient data.

Food photos (Wikimedia Commons)

Photos of Albanian foods come from Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org). Each photo is credited where it is shown full size: photographer, licence (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public domain) and a link to its page. Changes made by this app: the photos are resized and cropped to fit the screen. For some foods without a good free photo, the picture was made with AI (Google Gemini); these are marked “AI-generated picture” where they are shown full size.

German Nutrient Database (BLS)

Max Rubner-Institut (2025): Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS), Version 4.0 — Deutsche Nährstoffdatenbank (blsdb.de). Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes made by this app: carbohydrates are shown as total carbohydrates (available carbohydrates + fibre), and vitamin B6, copper and manganese are converted from µg to mg.

Ciqual French food composition table

Anses. 2025. Ciqual French food composition table (ciqual.anses.fr, doi:10.5281/zenodo.17550133). Licensed under CC BY 4.0 / Etalab Open Licence 2.0. Changes made by this app: carbohydrates are shown as total carbohydrates (carbohydrates + fibre); values given as “traces” or “< x” are shown as 0.

McCance and Widdowson's Composition of Foods (CoFID)

McCance and Widdowson's The Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset 2021 (Public Health England / Office for Health Improvement and Disparities, gov.uk). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Changes made by this app: carbohydrates are shown as total carbohydrates (available carbohydrates + fibre), at most 100 g per 100 g; vitamin A is calculated as retinol + carotene / 12; values given as “Tr” (trace) are shown as 0.

Canadian Nutrient File (CNF)

Canadian Nutrient File 2026, Health Canada (open.canada.ca). Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Values are shown as published; serving sizes given only as a weight (for example “100 g”) are left out because grams are always offered.

Formulas and targets

Calories at rest: Mifflin-St Jeor (1990) or Katch-McArdle. Body fat estimate: U.S. Navy method. Vitamin and mineral targets: U.S. Dietary Reference Intakes (National Academies). Sugar reference: EU reference intake. Water: U.S. National Academies.
